## 最终提示词

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent-background editorial still-life for the right side of a restrained Chinese calligraphy website. Create ONE ORIGINAL, genuinely photographic arrangement of the Four Treasures of the Study, not an illustration and not a 3D product render. Mood of a real calligrapher's modest desk, photographed for a fine traditional craft journal. A few overlapping thin warm ivory xuan paper sheets with subtle long fibers and naturally irregular soft edges; one slender used honey-colored bamboo Chinese brush with a dark ferrule, visibly individual tapered animal hairs and an ink-darkened pointed tip; one ordinary small worn rectangular charcoal-black inkstick with a very faint undecorated pressed border, lying flat, separate from the inkstone; one modest oval or rounded-rectangular dark purple-gray natural stone inkstone with a shallow grinding bed and a little freshly ground black ink. Realistic practical proportions: the brush is about twice the inkstone's length, paper broader than both. Keep the stone shape simple, unadorned and functional, inspired by real traditional inkstones rather than a futuristic bathtub. Paper occupies the upper-left and central part of the arrangement, inkstone lower-right, bamboo brush lying at a natural diagonal beside/across the edge of the sheets, inkstick flat next to the inkstone. The paper is blank except two or three quiet, irregular, authentic brush test strokes near a far corner; absolutely no readable words, labels, logos, decorative glyphs, seals or watermark. Soft overcast window daylight from upper left, delicate contact shadows, subdued warm-neutral colors, natural slight wear and imperfections, tactile matte stone with only the ink pool reflecting a small soft window highlight. Shot from a high three-quarter angle, approximately 65 degrees above the desk, 70mm lens, coherent perspective, sufficient depth of field for all objects, no dramatic bokeh. Wide compact grouping in a roughly 4:3 canvas, all four kinds of objects fully visible, comfortable 8% transparent padding around the outer silhouette. Genuine transparent alpha outside the paper and objects, including between separated objects. No room, no table surface rectangle, no beige backdrop, no gradients behind the cutout, no frames. Avoid fake smooth synthetic surfaces, thick puffy paper, sculpted plastic bristles, overly ornate carving, oversaturated gold, glossy CGI, theatrical light, floating objects, extra props, bamboo slips, ink bottles, porcelain, flowers or hands. It should look like a beautifully observed real still-life photograph, quiet and believable.
